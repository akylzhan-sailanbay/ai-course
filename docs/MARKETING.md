# Marketing assets: ready to produce

Everything here can be shot on a phone and a screen recorder. Text in quotes is ready to use; `[…]` marks facts
you fill in. Rules for every asset: **no income promises, no "become a developer in 4 weeks", no invented reviews
or numbers, no fake deadlines.** Every link carries a UTM tag or a bot payload so `/admin` can attribute it.

---

## 1. Messaging house

**Promise:** Первый сайт — за 60 минут. Свой продукт — за 4 недели.
**Positioning:** Learn to build useful real-world software with AI, even if you've never coded, and launch it safely.

| Pillar | What we say | Proof to show |
|---|---|---|
| Tangible results | 4 projects: lead site → booking app → Telegram bot → your own product | Screen recordings of each project |
| Speed | Live site in the first 60 minutes | The 60-minute build video; first-lesson clips |
| Accessible | Built for non-programmers; assistants 1:10 | Students' "before" (never coded) → their link |
| Safe launch | A whole week on keys, server checks, database rules, domains | "Leaked key" demo and its fix |
| Accountability | Live sessions, homework review, Demo Day, launch guarantee | Guarantee terms; Demo Day recordings |

**Words we use:** собрать, запустить, своя ссылка, заявка в Telegram, система записи, безопасно, вживую, Demo Day.
**Words we avoid:** «стань программистом», «пассивный доход», «заработай», «секретная методика», «последний шанс» (unless it's true).
**KZ voice:** «сіз», plain words, «ЖИ» for AI. Have a native speaker read every KZ asset.

## 4. Paid ads (Meta / Instagram)

Setup: objective **Leads/Conversions**, optimisation event **`QualifiedLead`** (custom conversion from the pixel)
once it has ~50 events a week; until then optimise for `CompleteRegistration`. Placements: Reels + Stories +
Feed. Audiences: (A) 25–40, KZ cities, interests in small business / entrepreneurship; (B) 25–40, interests in
ChatGPT / AI / freelancing. Separate RU and KZ ad sets. UTMs:
`?utm_source=instagram&utm_medium=cpc&utm_campaign={{campaign.name}}&utm_content={{ad.name}}`.

**Ad 1: Speed (video #1)**
- Primary: «Первый сайт — за 60 минут, даже если вы никогда не писали код. За 4 недели — сайт с заявками, система записи, Telegram-бот и ваш собственный проект. Вживую в Zoom, до 20 человек.»

**Ad 2: Local pain (video #2)**

## 7. Student-generated content (UGC) playbook

1. **Consent first:** in onboarding, ask for permission to record sessions and use clips/screens (a written yes in
   the chat is the minimum; add a consent clause to the offer).
2. **The first-hour moment:** at minute 55–60, ask volunteers to say on camera: «Час назад у меня не было сайта.
   Вот он: [ссылка]». Only use real reactions.
3. **Weekly showcase:** every Friday, students post their result in the chat; with permission, repost to the channel.
4. **Demo Day:** record 2-minute demos; cut a 10-second highlight per student.
5. **Testimonials:** ask 3 questions after Demo Day: «Что было до курса?», «Что вы собрали?», «Что бы сказали тому,
   кто сомневается?». Save the original message/video URL. The site only shows testimonials with a `sourceUrl`
   (see `course.testimonials`).

## 8. Link conventions (attribution)

| Where | Link |
|---|---|
| Instagram bio | `https://[domain]/?utm_source=instagram&utm_medium=bio` |
| Instagram ads | `…&utm_medium=cpc&utm_campaign=[name]&utm_content=[ad]` |
| Telegram channel post | `?utm_source=telegram&utm_medium=channel&utm_campaign=[post]` |
| Paid post in someone's channel | bot: `t.me/[bot]?start=s_tg-[channel]` or site: `?utm_source=tg-[channel]&utm_medium=paid_post` |
| Partner community | `?utm_source=partner-[name]&ref=[code]` |
| Student referral | `t.me/[bot]?start=r_[code]` (the bot gives each student their link via `/invite`) |
| Open lesson | `t.me/[bot]?start=s_openlesson` |

Bot payload format: `s_<source>[__<campaign>]`, lowercase letters, digits and hyphens, ≤ 30 characters each.
